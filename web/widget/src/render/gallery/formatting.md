# Settling in

Welcome. Here is what most residents ask about in their **first week**, with the *details* that matter and a few ~~old~~ updated notes.

## Before you arrive

1. Send your arrival time to the front desk.
2. Bring your passport and visa letter.
3. Read the house guide, which covers:
   - quiet hours, from `23:00` to `07:00`;
   - guests, who sign in at the desk;
   - the fire drill, held in the first month.

### Things to pack

- Bedding, unless you booked a starter pack
- A plug adapter (UK type G)
- [x] Visa letter printed
- [ ] Bank letter requested

#### Who to contact

Write to [the accommodation team](https://example.org/contact), email [help@example.org](mailto:help@example.org), or call [+44 20 7000 0000](tel:+442070000000). The full guide is at https://example.org/guide.
