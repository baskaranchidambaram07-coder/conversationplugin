# Design System: Conversation Plugin

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Familiar Window, Their Face"**

Three surfaces share one record, and all three are drawn in the PF design language (PFDL 2.3.0, its default Cobalt theme), Intellect's own design system: slate surfaces and inks, one cobalt primary, Roboto, flat surfaces with hairlines. The **chat** is the familiar AI chat window: a white page (deep navy in the dark), answers set as plain text beside the assistant's round mark, the visitor's words in a bubble of the primary colour, a bordered composer field at the foot. It wears the customer's identity in two ways: their assistant's picture (or logo, or initials) beside every answer, and their colour standing in for the primary. It refuses answer cards, brand-filled suggestion chips, a coloured header bar, a second accent, gradients, glass, and a shadow on anything that does not float.

The **launcher and panel frame** sit on someone else's page and take the least room they can: a 56px circle in the widget's colour or filled with the assistant's picture, an optional greeting card beside it, and a 14px-cornered panel in the chat's own ground that rises a short way and fades in. The **admin console** is a working admin in the same language: white cards on a slate canvas with one cobalt primary, in light and dark. The administrator chooses System, Light or Dark; System follows the computer.

Everything is Roboto, self-hosted, at modest sizes and weights; depth is hairlines first, shadow only where something floats.

This is an interim re-skin, and three gaps are known and accepted. The design language's component package (`@purple-fabric/ui`) cannot be installed yet, so each stylesheet writes out the token values at its top (the loader writes them as plain colours) and every part is still the product's own; the names in this document are the design language's token names, so the values can be dropped when the package supplies them. Status words stay capitalised ("Live", "In setup") although the design language writes them lowercase, because the same strings serve as headings and labels. The console's widget column heads its list with one all-caps group label ("Widgets", 12px, 600, 0.04em), which the design language allows for four words or fewer; it is that one heading, not a pattern for other surfaces.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Slate surfaces and inks in every surface (#ffffff cards, #f1f5f9 canvas, #0f172a ink), each with a dark value; one cobalt primary (#0047ab, #3870be in the dark).
- A widget's own colour stands in for the primary on the launcher, send, the caret and the visitor's bubble; send gives way to light ink on the dark composer when the colour is under 3:1.
- Answers are text, not cards; rich content (code, tables, diagrams, charts) sits in 12px hairline frames that scroll sideways inside themselves.
- Roboto throughout, 16px at 1.6 in the chat and 14px at 1.5 with tabular figures in the console; no running text under 12px.
- Flat surfaces with 1px hairlines; only the launcher, the greeting, the panel, the "latest message" circle, the console's account menu and its chat preview carry a shadow.
- Focus is one ring everywhere: 2px of the focus colour, 2px off.
- The chat picks light or dark by the widget's scheme, the console by the administrator's; both write each colour once with `light-dark()`.
- Logical properties throughout, so a right-to-left language mirrors.

## Colors

One slate family and one cobalt primary, shared by the chat, the loader and the console; status and chart colours carry meaning only.

### Primary
- **Cobalt Primary** (action-primary / action-primary-dark): the console's primary actions, done stations and the rail under them, radios, checkboxes, the field caret and the loading arc. In the chat it is the send button, the caret and the visitor's bubble when a widget has no colour of its own. Its hover (action-primary-hover) is the console's primary under the pointer. Text on it is text-on-primary.
- **Widget Colour** (runtime `--brand` and `--mine`, no fixed value): set per widget as `#` and six digits; when it is not valid, Cobalt Primary shows. The ink on it is white or black, whichever contrasts more (`inkOn`, computed, never configured).
- **Soft Primary Fill** (nav-active-bg): the current widget row, the chosen choice tile, pressed toggles, selected tabs, the current organisation, the current view and the console's selection; in the chat, the open chat's row and the current place. Text and glyphs on it take **Accent Text** (badge-count-text).
- **Focus** (focus-ring): the focus ring, and at 25% the stepper's halo.
- **Link** (text-link): links in a rendered answer.

### Tertiary (data and state)
- **Chart series** (viz-1 to viz-8, in that fixed order: blue, orange, teal, ochre, magenta, green, violet, red): the design language's eight series colours, each with a dark value except the ochre, which is the same in both. Data colours only; text never wears them.
- **State** (status-success, status-caution, status-danger and their dark values): green for live and passing, amber for live-with-unpublished-changes and the colour warnings, red for problems and failing checks. A widget still in setup takes the muted ink. Faults and failing checks sit on an 8% tint of the red over the surface; the shown-once secret on a 9% amber tint with a 30% amber hairline; the Live chip on status-success-bg.
- **Diagram inks** (diagram-body, diagram-quiet, diagram-frame-edge, diagram-note, diagram-note-edge): used only on a diagram's white sheet, with text-strong as the ink and surface-canvas as a subgraph's ground and a block's tag.

### Neutral
- **Canvas** (surface-canvas): the console's page and its secondary's hover; in the chat, the wide "Your chats" column.
- **Card** (surface-card): console cards, fields and the top bar; the chat's page, the panel frame before the chat loads, and inline code.
- **Raised** (surface-raised): the chat's composer, suggestion chips, secondary action, code, table, diagram and chart cards, the list of chats, the small cards it opens in place; the greeting. White in the light, a step above the page in the dark.
- **Neutral Fill** (surface-hover): the chat's one neutral fill: blockquotes, the initials mark, a file's thumbnail tile and a sent file's hover, the found-answer wash, the secondary action's hover, the greeting's dismiss under the pointer.
- **Hairline** (border-default): every 1px edge and rule, the table's header fill, the loading sketch bars, the ring's track and an upload's track.
- **Control Edge** (border-control): the edge of anything typed into: the composer, the chat's name field, every console field, the colour tile and the languages field. It holds 3:1 against the page.
- **Ink** (text-strong): text, a chosen thumb or pin (filled), an upload's progress line, the voice bars, and hover washes at 5 to 6%.
- **Muted** (text-muted): the AI notice, credit, placeholder, meta lines, quiet actions, header icons at rest, a pinned answer's question, a file's size, list markers, axis numbers, the working dots, hints.

### Named Rules
**The Stand-In Rule.** A widget's colour stands in for the primary and goes nowhere else: the launcher, the send button, the caret and the visitor's bubble. Elsewhere it appears only as a trace (a suggestion chip's edge at 55% and fill at 5% under the pointer, and the selection tint at 22%). Never on a header, a background, the mark or a chosen toggle.

**The 3:1 Rule.** On the dark composer (#1a2537) a widget colour under 3:1 gives way to the light ink (#f1f5f9) on the send button and the caret, and the ink on it is recomputed (`forDark`). The visitor's bubble is not a control, so it keeps the widget's colour in both schemes, with the ink that reads on it and an inner 1px edge of that ink at 16% for a colour close to the page's own. The console's colour notes use the same line: they warn when the colour is under 3:1 against the light composer (#ffffff) or the dark one, and state the text-on-colour ratio.

**The State-Only Rule.** Green, amber and red mean state (live, unpublished, problem) and nothing else. On the Soft Primary Fill a state colour takes a fifth of the ink (80% colour, 20% ink) to hold 4.5:1.

**The Light Sheet Rule.** A diagram keeps a white sheet in both schemes, with slate inks and frames; a drawn diagram keeps its source's own colours, which assume a light page.

## Typography

**Body Font:** Roboto Variable, self-hosted, upright in both apps and italic in the chat (with Roboto, then the platform's sans faces). The stack also names ten Noto Sans script faces for scripts Roboto lacks; none is shipped. The console adds tabular figures (`tnum`).
**Mono Font:** the platform's own monospace (ui-monospace, Cascadia Mono, SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas), used only for code and the hex field. The design language's PF Mono is not available, and nothing is downloaded in its place.
**Greeting Font:** the host page's Roboto if it has one, else its system face, because the greeting is drawn on the customer's page where our Roboto is not loaded.

**Character:** One neutral grotesque at modest sizes; hierarchy by weight before size. 700 is kept for page titles, the chat's welcome and the console's home figures; 600 for headings and buttons; 500 for labels, row titles and card titles.

### Hierarchy
- **Chat welcome** (700, 20px rising to 24px with the frame's width, 1.3, -0.017em, balanced, at most 28ch): an empty chat's first line, centred under the 52px mark.
- **Chat title** (500, 17px, 1.25, -0.01em): the header, and the "Your chats" heading; wraps anywhere.
- **Page title** (700, 20px to 24px, 1.25, -0.017em, balanced, wraps anywhere): the h2 of a page shown in the conversation's place (Projects, a project, Pinned).
- **Chat body** (400, 16px, 1.6): answers, the visitor's words, the composer (1.5 in the field).
- **Answer headings** (600, 1.3, balanced): h2 18px, h3 16px, h4 to h6 15px. A Markdown h1 renders as h2: the chat's title is the only h1.
- **Chat label** (400, 14px, 1.35): suggestion chips, meta and "unknown part" lines.
- **Chat action** (600, 13px): quiet and secondary actions; the code caption is the same in Muted.
- **Chat caption** (400, 12px): the character count.
- **Chat foot line** (400, 12px on 16px, Muted): the AI notice and the credit on one centred line, a middle dot between, whole from a 400px panel down to a 360px phone. Under 356px the credit moves under the notice and the dot goes; a notice too long for a line of its own ends in an ellipsis, its full text kept for screen readers and on hover.
- **Table** (400, 14.5px, 1.45, tabular figures), header cells 600. **Code** (mono, 13.5px, 1.6, tabs at 2).
- **Console page title** (700, 20px on a phone rising to 24px, 1.25, -0.017em, balanced). **Card title** (500, 17px, -0.01em): a station's card and a home panel. **Section title** (h3, 600, 15px).
- **Console body** (400, 14px, 1.5). **Label** (500, 14px): field labels, legends. **Action** (600, 14px): buttons. **Hint** (13px, Muted). **Small** (12px, Muted): state lines, minutes, table head cells, legends. **Figure** (700, 26px, 1.1, -0.02em, tabular): the home strip's counts.

### Named Rules
**The Pretty Wrap Rule.** Running text sets `text-wrap: pretty` and anything the customer or visitor supplies wraps anywhere. Code never wraps and table cells wrap only between words; both scroll sideways inside their own frame.

**The Twelve Floor Rule.** No running text is set under 12px, in the chat or the console.

## Layout

**Loader, chosen by window and host.**
- **Floating** (600px wide and 520px tall or more): the launcher 24px from the configured bottom corner (right by default, left by configuration), never inside the safe-area inset. The panel is `min(400px, 100vw - 48px)` by `min(640px, 100dvh - 120px)`, on the same side, 16px above the launcher; the open launcher becomes the close button. The greeting sits 12px above the closed launcher, at most 280px wide, and hides once the chat opens.
- **Full screen** (under 600px wide or 520px tall): the panel fills the viewport, no border, no radius; the launcher steps aside and the host page's scroll locks.
- **Expanded** (on the visitor's request, larger than a phone): the panel fills the window, no border or radius; closing returns to the panel.
- **Inline** (a host element): fills the target, at least 480px tall, no launcher, no shadow, no motion, no close button. From 720px of container width it drops border and radius.

**Chat.** One column: header (mark, title, actions; at least 60px), the scrolling conversation, then one foot landmark (composer, count, notice, credit). Gutter 16px, 10px under 340px wide, where the visitor's bubble may widen from 85% to 92% and the header's New chat hides when three actions would show. Turns stack 24px apart; rendered Markdown 10px; list items 4px with a 1.35em indent. The conversation fades over its top and bottom 14px (a mask, not an overlay). A "Go to the latest message" circle appears centred above the composer when the visitor scrolls up.
- **Wide frames (720px and wider):** the conversation, composer, notice and credit share one centred column of at most 760px; the visitor's bubble at most 75%. With history on, a 288px "Your chats" column runs the full height at the inline start on the Canvas with a hairline at its inline end, its chats as 12px-rounded rows with no card. An empty chat holds the welcome above the middle, the composer at the middle, the suggestions centred under it.
- **Narrow frames:** "Your chats" swaps the list in for the conversation in the same scrolling region, with no motion.
- **Places and pages (signed-in visitors):** the places (Projects, Pinned) sit above "Your chats" as rows 2px apart. A place opens as a page in the conversation's own scrolling region, never a dialog: its parts 14px apart, 10px clear of the top fade. An open chat carries its filing control at the top of the conversation.

**The Own Frame Rule.** Content wider than the column (code, tables, diagrams) scrolls sideways inside its own 12px frame, never the conversation. Each scroll region takes keyboard focus with its ring drawn inset. A soft shade shows at an inline edge only while more lies that way (a fixed radial of black at 22%, 12px, covered at each end by a 28px gradient of the frame's fill that scrolls with the content). Rows past the tenth fold behind "Show all N rows"; an expanded table scrolls vertically within `min(70vh, 560px)` with its header held.

**Console.** A top bar (at least 64px, 10px 28px, hairline under it), then a 280px widget column beside the work area (28px 40px 56px). The work area: page title, sites line, the six-station track with its sign at the end, then the station's card. Home shows the organisation's figures in one strip split by hairlines, then two panels side by side (one column under 1100px). On the four form stations the form sits beside the chat's live preview at 400px, 28px apart, the preview sticky 16px from the top. Forms stack 18px apart; field grids auto-fit columns of at least 240px. Under 1200px the preview stacks under the form and shows only on Look. Under 900px the column moves above the work, its widgets in a sideways-scrolling row of 200px tiles. Under 640px the bar becomes three rows, the track shows six dots with only the open station named, the figures fold to two columns and facts to one.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat surfaces, hairlines first. Inside the chat and the console, surfaces separate from the ground by a 1px edge in the hairline colour (drawn as a box-shadow so it never shifts layout), and fields by the darker control edge. Only what floats carries a shadow: on the host page, the launcher, the greeting and the panel; in the chat, the "latest message" circle; in the console, the account menu and the chat preview. The composer, cards, controls and the small cards the chat opens in place (the feedback comment, filing, a delete confirmation, file cards) are flat. Shadows are the design language's own (popover, menu, modal), neutral black, and deepen in the console's dark theme.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Popover** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.12)`): the "latest message" circle; with a `0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,.06)` edge, the launcher at rest.
- **Launcher hover** (`0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,.2), 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,.06)`, with a 2px lift; pointer only).
- **Greeting** (`0 10px 30px rgba(24,24,27,.14)`).
- **Menu** (`0 10px 30px rgba(24,24,27,.1)`, `rgba(0,0,0,.45)` in the dark, over a 1px hairline ring): the console's account menu.
- **Modal** (`0 24px 60px rgba(9,9,11,.22)`; `rgba(0,0,0,.6)` in the console's dark): the floating panel and the console's chat preview.

### Named Rules
**The Floats Only Rule.** A shadow means the thing floats over the page. Nothing that sits in the page (a card, a control, the composer) has one.

**The One Ring Rule.** Keyboard focus is a 2px ring of the focus colour, 2px off, and that geometry (a gap, then a ring) means focus and nothing else. Where an outline would be clipped or the focus belongs to a whole group (the composer, the chat's name field and comment card), the same ring is drawn as a box-shadow (`0 0 0 2px` of the card, `0 0 0 4px` of the focus colour) with a transparent outline for forced colours; inside a clipped card the outline is drawn 2px inset. On the host page the launcher and the greeting's buttons take 2px of white, then 2px of #1d4ed8, so the ring holds on any page and any colour.

## Shapes

The design language's corner scale, small and unshowy. Chat: the panel, composer and visitor bubble 14px; suggestion chips, code, table, diagram and chart frames, blockquotes, the list of chats, the pinned list, file cards and the feedback, filing and confirmation cards 12px; header icons, send, the attach clip, the microphone, place rows, the name field and the chart tip 10px; quiet and secondary actions, the answer toggles, filing options, file thumbnails and a file's remove, and the logo tile 8px; inline code and sketch bars 6px; the mark, launcher and "latest message" circle round. The greeting is 12px with its corner nearest the launcher pinched to 4px (mirrored when the launcher is on the left). Console: controls 8px, cards 12px, the chat preview 14px, choice tiles, snippets, faults, the add panel, the account menu and the changes table 10px, the segments' buttons 6px, the colour tile's swatch 5px, the Live chip fully round, station dots and state dots round. Glyphs are inline SVG on a 24 grid, 2px round strokes in `currentColor`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** read colours, corners and shadows from the design language's tokens (`--surface-card`, `--action-primary`, `--radius-card-lg`, `--shadow-popover`), written out at the top of each stylesheet until `@purple-fabric/ui` supplies them.
- **Do** let a widget's colour stand in for the primary on the launcher, send, the caret and the visitor's bubble, with white or black on it by contrast, and let send give way to light ink on the dark composer under 3:1.
- **Do** set answers as plain text beside the assistant's mark, and the visitor's words in a 14px bubble of the widget's colour with a faint inner edge.
- **Do** keep surfaces flat with 1px hairlines; put a shadow only under what floats (launcher, greeting, panel, the "latest message" circle, the account menu, the preview).
- **Do** give anything typed into the Control Edge (#77879d, #64748b in the dark), not the hairline.
- **Do** draw focus as the 2px ring of the focus colour, 2px off; as a box-shadow on the composer and the chat's small fields; as 2px white then 2px #1d4ed8 on anything drawn over the host page.
- **Do** mark the stepper's current station with a soft halo; a gap and a ring mean keyboard focus.
- **Do** give every colour a light and a dark value, written once with `light-dark()`.
- **Do** let wide code, tables and diagrams scroll inside their own 12px frame with the edge shade, never widening the conversation.
- **Do** start an answer's headings at h2 (18px, 600); the chat's title is the only h1.
- **Do** keep 700 for page titles, the welcome and the home figures; 600 for headings and buttons; 500 for labels, row and card titles.
- **Do** go full screen under 600px wide or 520px tall, with no radius and no border.
- **Do** keep green, amber and red for state alone, and mix a fifth of the ink into them on the Soft Primary Fill.
- **Do** fill a chosen toggle (a thumb, the pin) in Ink; pressed state never takes the widget's colour.
- **Do** open the chat's own small cards (feedback comment, filing, confirmation, files) flat, Raised with a 1px hairline and a 12px corner, and give any field in them the composer's focus.
- **Do** take chart series from the eight series colours in their order, and keep a diagram on its white sheet in both schemes.
- **Do** stop every animation while the panel is closed, and under reduced motion remove the rise, lift, press, spin and greeting animation, keeping only the fade; the dots and sketch bars hold still at 60%, and the found-answer wash still fades because it does not move.
- **Do** give every edge drawn as a box-shadow a 1px `CanvasText` border under forced colours, mark the current chat's row, place or filing option with a 2px `Highlight` outline, give a field that shows focus by box-shadow a transparent outline, and draw an upload's progress line in `CanvasText`.
- **Do** use logical properties, and mirror directional glyphs in right-to-left.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put answers on cards, fill suggestion chips with the widget's colour, or colour the chat's header.
- **Don't** put the widget's colour on a background, header, mark, chosen toggle or border at rest.
- **Don't** add a second accent, a gradient or glass.
- **Don't** put a shadow under a card, a control or the composer.
- **Don't** set text under 12px, or download a monospace face.
- **Don't** use rounded playful type or pill buttons in the console; it is 8px controls and 12px cards.
- **Don't** let text wear a chart's series colours; a series is named by its key.
- **Don't** stripe table rows.
- **Don't** bounce anything on entry.
